# Goal 3A — Required human actions

1. Log in to the NGII/국토정보플랫폼 account and download the Anyang DEM tiles covering the simulator boundary. Confirm tile completeness, CRS, vertical datum, nodata, grid spacing, and checksum.
2. Obtain permission and an export for KMA/official rainfall time series or radar covering the target events. Record station IDs, time zone, accumulation interval, missing-value handling, and redistribution terms.
3. Ask Anyang/utility data owners for drainage, sewer, inlet, stream/outfall, pump, storage, and boundary-condition data. Approve a redacted/public derivative if the full network cannot ship.
4. Supply at least two Anyang flood events with observed extents or depths, event timestamps, and a held-out event for validation. A screenshot or anecdote is not enough for Level B/C.
5. Resolve LarNO licensing by obtaining the repository license file and separate terms for weights and benchmark data. Until then, keep LarNO out of the product tree.
6. Approve the claim boundary: Goal 3B may expose only Level A relative scenario hazard unless the above evidence upgrades the maturity gate.
